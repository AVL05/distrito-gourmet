# Recursos visuales

La carta es tipográfica y no utiliza fotografías. Las imágenes gastronómicas se limitan a la portada.

## Pulpo a la brasa con parmentier trufado

Generado con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, a partir del plato existente en `DistritoGourmetSeeder.php` (id 3). La imagen es ilustrativa, no una fotografía de servicio.

- `frontend/public/pulpo-parmentier.webp`: 1536 × 1024.
- Variantes `pulpo-parmentier-640.webp` y `pulpo-parmentier-960.webp` para móviles y tabletas.
- Uso: sección «De nuestra carta» en `HomeView.jsx`.

Prompt utilizado:

> Create one photorealistic editorial food photograph for the homepage of Distrito Gourmet, a contemporary Spanish restaurant. The exact existing menu dish is 'Pulpo a la Brasa con Parmentier Trufado': a grilled rock octopus tentacle, silky potato purée with white truffle oil and pimentón de la Vera. Depict only this dish: one beautifully charred octopus tentacle with natural suckers on an understated ivory ceramic plate, silky ivory potato purée beneath, a restrained dusting of paprika and a subtle olive/truffle oil sheen. No garnish, no additional foods, no visible truffle slices. Set on a warm dark oak restaurant table, subtle black upholstery and out-of-focus brickwork behind, matching an urban Spanish industrial restaurant with dark metal pendant lights and exposed brick. Natural window light, authentic textures, warm restrained tones, appetizing realistic proportions, premium but simple plating. Landscape 3:2 composition, close three-quarter view, plate fully contained in frame with breathing room for responsive crop. No text, no logos, no people, no watermarks. This will be used only on homepage, not the formal menu.
